Privacy Policy

GameChest for iPhone, Android and the web · Last updated October 5, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Controller within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Benneth Müller, Benneth Müller Digital Solutions & Education, Borgfelder Str. 16, 20537 Hamburg, Germany, ben@gamechest.games. Contact for all privacy questions: ben@gamechest.games.

2. Short version

  • GameChest keeps a private list of video games you discover. Your collection is visible only to you, unless you share it by link (3.14).
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of your data, no analytics SDK of our own.
  • You can delete your account and your collection in the app at any time (Profile → Settings → Account & legal → Delete account).

3. What we process, why, and on which legal basis

3.1 Your account

When you create an account we process your email address, your password (stored by our hosting provider only in hashed form), an internal user ID and the display name you enter. You sign in with your email address and password; GameChest offers no sign-in through Apple, Google or other third-party accounts. Purpose: creating and securing your account and signing you in. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract).

3.2 Your collection and profile

We store what you save in GameChest: game titles, platform, genre, status, star rating, notes, the link or source where you found a game (without tracking parameters such as utm_ codes), for a shared post its title, author or channel name and preview image address, a scanned barcode, catalog references and cover image addresses, the date you saved an entry, your favourite genres, platforms and discovery sources, language, your room layout, your chosen companion and your showcase. Only you can read and change these entries. Purpose: providing the app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Game search and link lookup

When you search for a title, paste or share a link, confirm text read from a photo, or scan a barcode, GameChest sends this input to our server function. The function looks up matching games in public sources (a game store catalog, a game database, the public page or embed information of the shared link, and a barcode product database) and returns suggestions. It sends these sources only your input, never your name, email address or user ID. We do not store your search input with your account. To answer the same search faster, the server function keeps the search term (or the title it derived from your link or text) together with the catalog results in its working memory for at most 5 minutes, not linked to any account or user ID. Expired entries are deleted automatically, the memory holds only a limited number of recent terms, and a restart of the function clears it completely. Our server logs record only the type of request, its duration and its status, never the search text, link, text or barcode. The search behind the magnifying glass starts while you type (after a short pause and from two characters on), so a search term is sent before you press Enter; a pasted link is only sent when you confirm it. The search field in your Chest tab only filters the games you already saved; this happens on your device and sends nothing. Only when you save a game do the details you confirm become part of your collection (3.2). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Link answers we keep for everyone. So that a link many players share is not looked up again each time, the server function keeps the answer for a shared link in a database table: the link without tracking parameters and the suggested games (titles, platforms, catalog numbers, the link's public title and preview picture address). The text you shared with the link is not stored, only used inside a hash that forms the key. The entry is not linked to any account, user ID or IP address and is deleted after 7 days at the latest (after 24 hours when it contains data from the game database IGDB or a Twitch clip); links to Twitch channels and empty answers are never kept. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in fast answers and lower costs).

Daily count of free checks. From app version 1.0.3, a check of a link or barcode without GameChest Plus counts against a daily number of free checks. For this we store, per account and day (UTC), only a number, no link, no text and no result. When the number is reached, our server asks RevenueCat (3.6) whether you have Plus. The counts are deleted after 7 days and immediately when you delete your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Transcript of a video. From app version 1.0.3, when a shared video link finds no game in its title, description or page, the server function may ask the transcript service Supadata for the spoken words of that public video (only the link is sent, never your identity) and looks for a game name in it. This only happens with GameChest Plus or within your free daily checks. The transcript is kept in working memory for at most 5 minutes and not stored. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

AI check of the suggestions. Before the suggestions come back, our server function has them checked by the AI model Claude from Anthropic (same provider and terms as in 3.10: Anthropic Ireland, Limited; processing also in the USA; no training on this data under Anthropic's Commercial Terms; deletion by Anthropic within 30 days, longer only if flagged for violating its Usage Policy). This happens for every search, link, photo text and barcode, without a separate consent, because it is part of how the search works. Anthropic receives only your input (the search term; the text read from a photo; the product name the barcode database returned; for a link its title, description, the text you shared with it and the website's domain, never the full address) together with up to eight catalog suggestions (title, platforms, year). It never receives your name, email address or user ID. Anthropic returns which suggestions fit and in what order, and at most one game title that we then look up in the game database. If a link or text finds no game, the server function may also ask the same model which game it mentions, with the same data and terms. So that the same input is not checked twice, the server function reuses the result for up to 5 minutes from its working memory, not linked to any account or user ID; the memory holds only a limited number of entries and a restart clears it. If the AI check fails or takes longer than 4 seconds, you get the suggestions without it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.4 Photos, camera and on-device text recognition

If you allow camera or photo access, GameChest reads barcodes and text from the picture on your device; text recognition uses Google ML Kit. The image is not uploaded, unless you ask the photo AI to recognise it (3.12). Google ML Kit does, however, send technical metrics about the use and performance of the recognition feature to Google (for example device model, operating system and app version, a per-installation identifier, latency, feature settings, event types and error codes). According to Google these metrics are used for diagnostics and usage analytics and are not linked to your identity. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is offering reliable on-device text recognition. If you do not use the photo or scan features, no images are processed.

3.5 Cover images

Game covers are loaded by your device directly from the image servers of the game catalogs. Like any web request, this transmits your IP address to those servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (showing the covers you asked for).

The same applies to the preview image of a shared post (for example from Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X or Twitch): your device loads it directly from that platform's image server, and only while the source card is on screen. We do not upload the image and do not pass it on to anyone else.

3.6 GameChest Plus subscription

Purchases are made and paid through Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details. To unlock Plus and to restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase and subscription information (products, dates, status, transaction receipts) together with your GameChest user ID, or an anonymous ID in the demo. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We also use RevenueCat's dashboard to evaluate subscriptions in aggregate (for example number of subscribers, trials, renewals and revenue); legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in running the subscription business. Deleting your GameChest account does not cancel a running subscription; you cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

Free tries and usage counter: without Plus, your account can try three functions three times each: adding a game recognised from a link or post (link import), adding a game recognised from a photo or barcode (camera), and an answer from Till (3.10). The three tries count once per account, not per device and not per day. After that the app shows the Plus offer. Searching for games by name and saving, finishing, rating and sharing games stay free and unlimited. With Plus, link import and camera are unlimited; Till is subject to fair use of up to 150 answers per calendar month. To count this, our server stores one entry per counted use in a usage counter: your user ID, the function (link import, camera, Till, or Till with Plus), the ID of the use (for a saved game the ID of its new entry in your collection, for Till a random ID), the period (the calendar month for Till with Plus, none for the free tries) and the time. The counter contains no links, photos, text or game titles. A use that fails (the game could not be saved, Till gave no answer) is given back and not counted. In the demo nothing is counted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Storage period: section 5.

3.7 Data stored only on your device

GameChest stores settings (for example sound and music, tour progress), demo data, discoveries you shared to GameChest from other apps (links, text or screenshots, through the share extension on iPhone or the share menu on Android; kept until you review or discard them, a shared screenshot is read on your device and never uploaded), and games and links you saved for later when a function's free tries were used up (3.6; at most 50, stored for your account on this device and deleted with your account). None of this leaves your device unless you actively use the related feature. When you paste a link, GameChest checks the clipboard only while the empty search field is active in order to offer "Paste link". Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.8 Server logs

When the app connects to our servers, technical connection data (such as IP address, time and requested address) is processed by our hosting provider to operate and secure the service. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security and stability). Retention follows the hosting provider's log retention: up to 7 days, after which the logs are deleted automatically. The website gamechest.games is hosted on Netlify (Netlify, Inc., USA); when you open it, Netlify receives your IP address and processes it in its server logs to deliver and secure the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.9 Emails

We send emails only for your account (confirmation, password reset). No newsletters. Our sign-in service at Supabase creates these emails; they are delivered through the SMTP mail server of Hostinger International Ltd., which receives your email address and the content of the email (for example the confirmation link) to deliver it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.10 Till, the game advisor (AI)

Till is an AI, not a person. He answers with the AI model Claude from Anthropic (Claude API; contracting party Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Ireland; Anthropic may process the data in the USA and other countries and stores it in the USA). Only after you agree in the chat do we send, through our server, your message, the last messages of the current conversation, the titles, platforms and statuses of the games in your chest and the platforms and genres from your profile to Anthropic, together with game data from the IGDB catalog. We do not send your name, email address, notes or ratings. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, Anthropic does not train its models on this data. Anthropic processes it as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and deletes it within 30 days. If a message is flagged for violating Anthropic's Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep it for up to 2 years and the safety classification for up to 7 years. AI answers can be wrong or out of date. Please check facts, prices and age ratings before you rely on them.

Using Till safely: Till only talks about games and is instructed to keep answers suitable for players aged 13 and over. Do not share personal details with him, such as your real name, address, school or phone number. If an answer seems wrong or inappropriate, long-press it in the chat and choose “Report answer”. We then store the reported answer (only Till's answer, not your messages) with your user ID and the time for up to 180 days to check it. Till's answers are counted in the usage counter described in 3.6 (three answers to try without Plus, fair use of up to 150 answers per calendar month with Plus). Daily answer counters from earlier versions (user ID, date, number of answers) are deleted by a daily job once they are older than 31 days. The conversation itself is not stored on our servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent in the chat) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for reports also Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in keeping Till safe). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): in the app under Settings → AI advisor (Till): Consent → Withdraw consent. From then on Till sends nothing more to Anthropic (the AI check of search suggestions in 3.3 is separate), and Till asks for your consent again the next time you open him. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. In the demo, Till answers on your device without AI.

3.11 Web app (gamechest.games/app)

GameChest can also be used in the browser at gamechest.games/app. The web app is hosted on Netlify (Netlify, Inc., USA). When you open it, Netlify receives your IP address and processes it together with the time and the requested address in its server logs to deliver and secure the site; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The web app uses the same services as the app (Supabase for your account and collection, the game catalogs for search and covers, Anthropic Claude for the AI check of search suggestions and, after your consent, for Till), as described above. It offers no purchases, no sharing from other apps, no barcode scanning and no photos, so RevenueCat, the store payment services and ML Kit are not involved. Your sign-in (session token) and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage; this is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to provide the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Signing out removes the sign-in, and you can delete the rest through your browser's site data. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising cookies. To display characters that the app's own fonts do not contain (for example emoji or Japanese titles), your browser may load a matching font file from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, Google LLC, USA), which transmits your IP address to Google; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (displaying text correctly).

3.12 Photo AI (recognising a cover with AI)

If the text read from a photo on your device finds no game, the app on iPhone and Android can offer “Recognise with AI”. Photo AI is part of GameChest Plus (3.6): without Plus, tapping it only opens the Plus offer; no consent is asked and nothing is sent. Without Plus, photos are read only on your device (3.4). The photo itself is never sent automatically: only when you tap it with Plus, and only after you agree in the dialog the first time, do we send, through our server function, a reduced copy of this one photo (at most 1024 pixels on its longest side, as JPEG, with all EXIF data such as location, time and camera model removed on your device) and the text read from it to the AI model Claude from Anthropic (same provider and terms as in 3.10: Anthropic Ireland, Limited; processing also in the USA; no training on this data under Anthropic's Commercial Terms; deletion by Anthropic within 30 days, longer only if flagged for violating its Usage Policy). Anthropic returns up to three game titles, which the app then searches in the game catalogs like a typed title (3.3). We do not store the photo or its text, neither in a database nor with your account, and our server logs never contain the photo, the text or the recognised titles. To keep the daily limit we store a counter per day (user ID, date, number of photos); a daily job deletes counters older than 31 days, and they are deleted immediately with your account. A game you then add counts as a camera use (3.6). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent in the app). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): in the app under Settings → Photo AI: Consent → Withdraw consent. From then on no photo is sent, and the app asks again before the next one. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. The photo AI is not available in the demo or in the web app.

3.13 Game details (playtime, screenshots, trailers)

When you open a game from the IGDB catalog, the app asks our server function for extra details such as playtime, screenshots and trailer links. The function gets them from IGDB (Twitch Interactive, Inc.) and sends IGDB only the game's catalog number, never your name, email address or user ID. Our server logs record only the type of request, its duration and its status, never which game you opened. Screenshots are loaded by your device directly from IGDB's image server, which receives your IP address (as with cover images, 3.5). In the demo and without a sign-in, no details are requested. A trailer opens outside GameChest on YouTube (in the YouTube app or your browser) only when you tap it; from then on Google's terms and privacy policy apply (policies.google.com/privacy). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.14 Sharing your chest by link

When you tap Share and choose a link, the app sends our server function a copy of the chest you share: the chest's name and, per game, its title, platform, status, your star rating, the cover image address and the catalog number. Notes, your email address and your user ID are not part of this copy. We store the copy together with your user ID (so only you can withdraw it) and create a short link such as gamechest.games/c/K7Q2XM9P.

Anyone who has the link can see the shared chest, without the app and without an account; the page is not listed in search engines. The link works for 30 days. You can withdraw it earlier at any time in the app; the stored copy is then deleted immediately and the link shows nothing. A link is also withdrawn when you delete your account. The stored copy is deleted at the latest 30 days after the link expired, and immediately when you withdraw it or delete your account. The copy does not change when you change your collection later; share again for a new one.

When someone opens the link, the page (Netlify, 3.11) loads the chest from our server function at Supabase; the function uses the visitor's IP address only in working memory, for at most 10 minutes, to limit the number of requests, and does not store it; like every request to our servers, it appears in the hosting provider's technical logs (3.8). Cover images are loaded directly from the image servers of the game catalogs (IGDB, Steam), which receive the visitor's IP address (as in 3.5). Our own function logs record only the type of request, its duration and its status, never the link, the chest or who opened it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you ask us to share your chest).

3.15 Several finds per game

From app version 1.0.5, when you share a second post about a game that is already in your chest, you can keep it as an extra find. We store it with the game in your collection: the link, the platform, the post's public title and author name, its preview picture address and, for a live stream, the stream number, the channel name and the time you shared it. At most 10 extra finds are kept per game; older ones are removed. They are deleted with the game and immediately when you delete your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.16 Votes by link ("Which one tonight?")

From app version 1.0.5, you can let friends vote which of 2 to 4 of your games you should play. The app sends our server function the chosen games (title, platform, cover image address, catalog number) and we create a short link such as gamechest.games/v/K7Q2XM9P. The vote is stored with your user ID (so only you can end it), but your name and email address are not shown. Anyone who has the link can vote without an account. A voter's browser creates a random number that is stored in the browser; we store only a keyed hash of it and, to stop repeated votes, a keyed hash of the voter's IP address. We never store the IP address itself. The IP hashes are deleted after 24 hours; the vote and all its ballots are deleted 7 days after it ended, and immediately when you delete your account. A vote ends after 48 hours or when you end it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you ask us to run the vote) and, for the voters' hashes, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in fair votes without spam).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, collection and profile: until you delete your account. Deletion in the app removes your profile, your collection and your sign-in account from our servers immediately; it cannot be undone.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: when you delete your account in the app, our server asks RevenueCat to delete your RevenueCat customer record (your GameChest user ID with its purchase and subscription history). If the request is not made or fails, your account is still deleted, and our server notes the open request (only your GameChest user ID) and repeats it automatically every 6 hours for up to 7 days. The note is removed 30 days after RevenueCat confirmed the deletion. If the record still cannot be deleted automatically, we delete it at RevenueCat by hand; you can also write to us and we will do so within 30 days. Deleting the account does not cancel a running subscription (3.6). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
  • Usage counter for link import, camera and Till (3.6): the entries for the three free tries are kept until you delete your account, because they count once per account. Entries for Till with Plus (per calendar month) are deleted by a daily job once they are older than the previous calendar month. Everything is deleted immediately when you delete your account.
  • Till's daily answer counters from earlier versions: deleted by a daily job once they are older than 31 days, and immediately when you delete your account (3.10).
  • Search input: not stored with your account; the search term and its catalog results stay in the server function's working memory for at most 5 minutes and are then deleted (3.3).
  • Link answers: not linked to any account; deleted after 7 days at the latest, after 24 hours when they contain IGDB or Twitch data (3.3).
  • Daily count of free checks: deleted after 7 days, and immediately when you delete your account (3.3).
  • Extra finds per game: until you remove the game or delete your account; at most 10 per game (3.15).
  • Votes: voters' IP hashes after 24 hours; the vote with all ballots 7 days after it ended, and immediately when you delete your account (3.16).
  • Till's reported answers: up to 180 days, deleted earlier with your account. Anthropic deletes chat data within 30 days (3.10).
  • Photo AI: the photo and its text are not stored; the daily photo counter is deleted by a daily job once it is older than 31 days, and immediately when you delete your account (3.12).
  • Shared chest links: 30 days, or until you withdraw them or delete your account; the stored copy is deleted at the latest 30 days after expiry, and immediately when you withdraw it or delete your account (3.14).
  • Data on your device: until you sign out, delete the demo data or delete the app.

3.3 Spielsuche und Linkauflösung

Wenn du nach einem Titel suchst, einen Link einfügst oder teilst, einen aus einem Foto gelesenen Text bestätigst oder einen Barcode scannst, sendet GameChest diese Eingabe an unsere Serverfunktion. Sie sucht passende Spiele in öffentlichen Quellen (einem Spiele-Shop-Katalog, einer Spieledatenbank, den öffentlichen Seiten- bzw. Einbettungsangaben des geteilten Links und einer Barcode-Produktdatenbank) und liefert Vorschläge zurück. An diese Quellen geht nur deine Eingabe, nie dein Name, deine E-Mail-Adresse oder deine Benutzer-ID. Deine Sucheingaben speichern wir nicht zu deinem Konto. Wird die Anfrage nicht gestellt oder schlägt sie fehl, wird dein Konto trotzdem gelöscht; unser Server merkt sich die offene Anfrage (nur deine GameChest-Benutzer-ID) und wiederholt sie automatisch alle 6 Stunden, höchstens 7 Tage lang. Der Vermerk wird 30 Tage nach der bestätigten Löschung bei RevenueCat entfernt. Lässt sich der Datensatz auch so nicht löschen, löschen wir ihn bei RevenueCat von Hand; du kannst uns auch schreiben, dann tun wir das innerhalb von 30 Tagen. Das Löschen des Kontos kündigt ein laufendes Abo nicht (3.6). Apple und Google bewahren eigene Kaufbelege nach eigenen Regeln auf.
  • Nutzungszähler für Link-Import, Kamera und Till (3.6): Die Einträge der drei Gratis-Tests bleiben, bis du dein Konto löschst, weil sie einmal pro Konto gelten. Einträge für Till mit Plus (je Kalendermonat) löscht ein täglicher Job, sobald sie älter als der vorige Kalendermonat sind. Mit deinem Konto wird alles sofort gelöscht.
  • Tills Tageszähler aus früheren Versionen: ein täglicher Job löscht sie, sobald sie älter als 31 Tage sind, und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst (3.10).
  • Sucheingaben: nicht zu deinem Konto gespeichert; Suchbegriff und Katalogtreffer bleiben höchstens 5 Minuten im Arbeitsspeicher der Serverfunktion und werden dann gelöscht (3.3).
  • Link-Antworten: mit keinem Konto verknüpft; spätestens nach 7 Tagen gelöscht, nach 24 Stunden, wenn sie IGDB- oder Twitch-Daten enthalten (3.3).
  • Tageszählung der kostenlosen Prüfungen: nach 7 Tagen gelöscht und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst (3.3).
  • Weitere Funde je Spiel: bis du das Spiel entfernst oder dein Konto löschst; höchstens 10 je Spiel (3.15).
  • Abstimmungen: IP-Hashes der Abstimmenden nach 24 Stunden; die Abstimmung mit allen Stimmen 7 Tage nach ihrem Ende und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst (3.16).
  • Von dir gemeldete Till-Antworten: bis zu 180 Tage, mit deinem Konto früher gelöscht. Anthropic löscht Chatdaten binnen 30 Tagen (3.10).

  • Geteilte Truhen-Links: 30 Tage bzw. bis du sie widerrufst oder dein Konto löschst; die gespeicherte Kopie wird spätestens 30 Tage nach Ablauf gelöscht, bei Widerruf oder Kontolöschung sofort (3.14).
