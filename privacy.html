Privacy Policy

GameChest for iPhone, Android and the web · Last updated September 29, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Controller within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Benneth Müller, Benneth Müller Digital Solutions & Education, Borgfelder Str. 16, 20537 Hamburg, Germany, ben@gamechest.games. Contact for all privacy questions: ben@gamechest.games.

2. Short version

  • GameChest keeps a private list of video games you discover. Your collection is visible only to you.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of your data, no analytics SDK of our own.
  • You can delete your account and your collection in the app at any time (Profile → Settings → Account & legal → Delete account).

3. What we process, why, and on which legal basis

3.1 Your account

When you create an account we process your email address, your password (stored by our hosting provider only in hashed form), an internal user ID and the display name you enter. You sign in with your email address and password; GameChest offers no sign-in through Apple, Google or other third-party accounts. Purpose: creating and securing your account and signing you in. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract).

3.2 Your collection and profile

We store what you save in GameChest: game titles, platform, genre, status, star rating, notes, the link or source where you found a game (without tracking parameters such as utm_ codes), for a shared post its title, author or channel name and preview image address, a scanned barcode, catalog references and cover image addresses, the date you saved an entry, your favourite genres, platforms and discovery sources, language, your room layout, your chosen companion and your showcase. Only you can read and change these entries. Purpose: providing the app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Game search and link lookup

When you search for a title, paste or share a link, confirm text read from a photo, or scan a barcode, GameChest sends this input to our server function. The function looks up matching games in public sources (a game store catalog, a game database, the public page or embed information of the shared link, and a barcode product database) and returns suggestions. It sends these sources only your input, never your name, email address or user ID. We do not store your search input in a database or with your account. To answer the same search faster, the server function keeps the search term (or the title it derived from your link or text) together with the catalog results in its working memory for at most 5 minutes, not linked to any account or user ID. Expired entries are deleted automatically, the memory holds only a limited number of recent terms, and a restart of the function clears it completely. Our server logs record only the type of request, its duration and its status, never the search text, link, text or barcode. The search behind the magnifying glass starts while you type (after a short pause and from two characters on), so a search term is sent before you press Enter; a pasted link is only sent when you confirm it. The search field in your Chest tab only filters the games you already saved; this happens on your device and sends nothing. Only when you save a game do the details you confirm become part of your collection (3.2). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.4 Photos, camera and on-device text recognition

If you allow camera or photo access, GameChest reads barcodes and text from the picture on your device; text recognition uses Google ML Kit. The image is not uploaded, unless you ask the photo AI to recognise it (3.12). Google ML Kit does, however, send technical metrics about the use and performance of the recognition feature to Google (for example device model, operating system and app version, a per-installation identifier, latency, feature settings, event types and error codes). According to Google these metrics are used for diagnostics and usage analytics and are not linked to your identity. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is offering reliable on-device text recognition. If you do not use the photo or scan features, no images are processed.

3.5 Cover images

Game covers are loaded by your device directly from the image servers of the game catalogs. Like any web request, this transmits your IP address to those servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (showing the covers you asked for).

The same applies to the preview image of a shared post (for example from Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X or Twitch): your device loads it directly from that platform's image server, and only while the source card is on screen. We do not upload the image and do not pass it on to anyone else.

3.6 GameChest Plus subscription

Purchases are made and paid through Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details. To unlock Plus and to restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase and subscription information (products, dates, status, transaction receipts) together with your GameChest user ID, or an anonymous ID in the demo. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We also use RevenueCat's dashboard to evaluate subscriptions in aggregate (for example number of subscribers, trials, renewals and revenue); legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in running the subscription business. Deleting your GameChest account does not cancel a running subscription; you cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

3.7 Data stored only on your device

GameChest stores settings (for example sound and music, tour progress), demo data, discoveries you shared to GameChest from other apps (links, text or screenshots, through the share extension on iPhone or the share menu on Android; kept until you review or discard them, a shared screenshot is read on your device and never uploaded). None of this leaves your device unless you actively use the related feature. When you paste a link, GameChest checks the clipboard only while the empty search field is active in order to offer "Paste link". Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.8 Server logs

When the app connects to our servers, technical connection data (such as IP address, time and requested address) is processed by our hosting provider to operate and secure the service. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security and stability). Retention follows the hosting provider's log retention: up to 7 days, after which the logs are deleted automatically. The website gamechest.games is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA); when you open it, GitHub receives your IP address and processes it in its server logs to deliver and secure the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.9 Emails

We send emails only for your account (confirmation, password reset). No newsletters. Our sign-in service at Supabase creates these emails; they are delivered through the SMTP mail server of Hostinger International Ltd., which receives your email address and the content of the email (for example the confirmation link) to deliver it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.10 Till, the game advisor (AI)

Till is an AI, not a person. He answers with the AI model Claude from Anthropic (Claude API; contracting party Anthropic Ireland, Limited, Ireland; Anthropic may process the data in the USA and other countries and stores it in the USA). Only after you agree in the chat do we send, through our server, your message, the last messages of the current conversation, the titles, platforms and statuses of the games in your chest and the platforms and genres from your profile to Anthropic, together with game data from the IGDB catalog. We do not send your name, email address, notes or ratings. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, Anthropic does not train its models on this data. Anthropic processes it as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and deletes it within 30 days. If a message is flagged for violating Anthropic's Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep it for up to 2 years and the safety classification for up to 7 years. AI answers can be wrong or out of date. Please check facts, prices and age ratings before you rely on them.

Using Till safely: Till only talks about games and is instructed to keep answers suitable for players aged 13 and over. Do not share personal details with him, such as your real name, address, school or phone number. If an answer seems wrong or inappropriate, long-press it in the chat and choose “Report answer”. We then store the reported answer (only Till's answer, not your messages) with your user ID and the time for up to 180 days to check it. To keep the daily answer limit we store a counter per day (user ID, date, number of answers); a daily job deletes counters older than 31 days. The conversation itself is not stored on our servers. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent in the chat) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for reports also Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in keeping Till safe). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): in the app under Settings → AI advisor (Till): Consent → Withdraw consent. From then on nothing is sent to Anthropic, and Till asks for your consent again the next time you open him. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. In the demo, Till answers on your device without AI.

3.11 Web app (gamechest.games/app)

GameChest can also be used in the browser at gamechest.games/app. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company, USA). When you open it, GitHub receives your IP address and processes it together with the time and the requested address in its server logs to deliver and secure the site; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The web app uses the same services as the app (Supabase for your account and collection, the game catalogs for search and covers, Anthropic Claude for Till after your consent), as described above. It offers no purchases, no sharing from other apps, no barcode scanning and no photos, so RevenueCat, the store payment services and ML Kit are not involved. Your sign-in (session token) and your settings are kept in your browser's local storage; this is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to provide the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Signing out removes the sign-in, and you can delete the rest through your browser's site data. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising cookies. To display characters that the app's own fonts do not contain (for example emoji or Japanese titles), your browser may load a matching font file from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, Google LLC, USA), which transmits your IP address to Google; legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (displaying text correctly).

3.12 Photo AI (recognising a cover with AI)

If the text read from a photo on your device finds no game, the app on iPhone and Android can offer “Recognise with AI”. Nothing is sent automatically: only when you tap it, and only after you agree in the dialog the first time, do we send, through our server function, a reduced copy of this one photo (at most 1024 pixels on its longest side, as JPEG, with all EXIF data such as location, time and camera model removed on your device) and the text read from it to the AI model Claude from Anthropic (same provider and terms as in 3.10: Anthropic Ireland, Limited; processing also in the USA; no training on this data under Anthropic's Commercial Terms; deletion by Anthropic within 30 days, longer only if flagged for violating its Usage Policy). Anthropic returns up to three game titles, which the app then searches in the game catalogs like a typed title (3.3). We do not store the photo or its text, neither in a database nor with your account, and our server logs never contain the photo, the text or the recognised titles. To keep the daily limit we store a counter per day (user ID, date, number of photos); a daily job deletes counters older than 31 days, and they are deleted immediately with your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent in the app). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): in the app under Settings → Photo AI: Consent → Withdraw consent. From then on no photo is sent, and the app asks again before the next one. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. The photo AI is not available in the demo or in the web app.

3.13 Game details (playtime, screenshots, trailers)

When you open a game from the IGDB catalog, the app asks our server function for extra details such as playtime, screenshots and trailer links. The function gets them from IGDB (Twitch Interactive, Inc.) and sends IGDB only the game's catalog number, never your name, email address or user ID. Our server logs record only the type of request, its duration and its status, never which game you opened. Screenshots are loaded by your device directly from IGDB's image server, which receives your IP address (as with cover images, 3.5). In the demo and without a sign-in, no details are requested. A trailer opens outside GameChest on YouTube (in the YouTube app or your browser) only when you tap it; from then on Google's terms and privacy policy apply (policies.google.com/privacy). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, collection and profile: until you delete your account. Deletion in the app removes your profile, your collection and your sign-in account from our servers immediately; it cannot be undone.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: when you delete your account in the app, our server asks RevenueCat to delete your RevenueCat customer record (your GameChest user ID with its purchase and subscription history). If the request is not made or fails, your account is still deleted, and our server notes the open request (only your GameChest user ID) and repeats it automatically every 6 hours for up to 7 days. The note is removed 30 days after RevenueCat confirmed the deletion. If the record still cannot be deleted automatically, we delete it at RevenueCat by hand; you can also write to us and we will do so within 30 days. Deleting the account does not cancel a running subscription (3.6). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
  • Till's daily answer counter: deleted by a daily job once it is older than 31 days, and immediately when you delete your account (3.10).
  • Search input: not stored in a database or with your account; the search term and its catalog results stay in the server function's working memory for at most 5 minutes and are then deleted (3.3).
  • Till's reported answers: up to 180 days, deleted earlier with your account. Anthropic deletes chat data within 30 days (3.10).
  • Photo AI: the photo and its text are not stored; the daily photo counter is deleted by a daily job once it is older than 31 days, and immediately when you delete your account (3.12).
  • Data on your device: until you sign out, delete the demo data or delete the app.

3.12 Foto-KI (Cover mit KI erkennen)

Findet der auf deinem Gerät aus einem Foto gelesene Text kein Spiel, kann die App auf iPhone und Android „Mit KI erkennen“ anbieten. Automatisch wird nichts gesendet: Erst wenn du darauf tippst, und beim ersten Mal erst nach deiner Zustimmung im Dialog, übermitteln wir über unsere Server-Funktion eine verkleinerte Kopie dieses einen Fotos (höchstens 1024 Pixel an der längsten Seite, als JPEG, alle EXIF-Daten wie Standort, Zeitpunkt und Kameramodell schon auf deinem Gerät entfernt) und den darauf gelesenen Text an das KI-Modell Claude von Anthropic (derselbe Anbieter und dieselben Bedingungen wie in 3.10: Anthropic Ireland, Limited; Verarbeitung auch in den USA; nach Anthropics Commercial Terms kein Training mit diesen Daten; Löschung bei Anthropic innerhalb von 30 Tagen, länger nur bei einer Markierung wegen eines Verstoßes gegen die Nutzungsrichtlinie). Anthropic gibt bis zu drei Spieltitel zurück, die die App dann wie einen eingetippten Titel in den Spielekatalogen sucht (3.3). Das Foto und seinen Text speichern wir nicht, weder in einer Datenbank noch bei deinem Konto, und unsere Server-Logs enthalten nie das Foto, den Text oder die erkannten Titel. Für das Tageslimit speichern wir je Tag einen Zähler (Benutzer-ID, Datum, Anzahl der Fotos); ein täglicher Job löscht Zähler, die älter als 31 Tage sind, und mit deinem Konto werden sie sofort gelöscht. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO (deine Einwilligung in der App). Deine Einwilligung kannst du jederzeit mit Wirkung für die Zukunft widerrufen (Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO): in der App unter Settings → Photo AI: Consent → Withdraw consent (auf Deutsch: Einstellungen → Foto-KI: Einwilligung → Einwilligung widerrufen). Danach wird kein Foto mehr gesendet, und die App fragt vor dem nächsten erneut. Die Rechtmäßigkeit der Verarbeitung bis zum Widerruf bleibt unberührt. In der Demo und in der Web-App gibt es die Foto-KI nicht.

3.13 Spieldetails (Spielzeit, Screenshots, Trailer)

Öffnest du ein Spiel aus dem IGDB-Katalog, fragt die App unsere Serverfunktion nach zusätzlichen Angaben wie Spielzeit, Screenshots und Trailer-Links. Die Funktion holt sie bei IGDB (Twitch Interactive, Inc.) und sendet IGDB dafür nur die Katalognummer des Spiels, nie deinen Namen, deine E-Mail-Adresse oder deine Benutzer-ID. Unsere Server-Logs halten nur Art, Dauer und Status der Anfrage fest, nie welches Spiel du geöffnet hast. Screenshots lädt dein Gerät direkt vom Bildserver von IGDB, der dabei deine IP-Adresse erhält (wie bei den Cover-Bildern, 3.5). In der Demo und ohne Anmeldung werden keine Details abgefragt. Einen Trailer öffnet GameChest erst, wenn du ihn antippst, und zwar außerhalb der App bei YouTube (in der YouTube-App oder deinem Browser); ab dann gelten Googles Bedingungen und Datenschutzerklärung (policies.google.com/privacy). Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO.

5. Speicherdauer

  • Konto, Sammlung und Profil: bis du dein Konto löschst. Die Löschung in der App entfernt Profil, Sammlung und Anmeldekonto sofort von unseren Servern; sie lässt sich nicht rückgängig machen.
  • Abo-Daten bei RevenueCat: Wenn du dein Konto in der App löschst, bittet unser Server RevenueCat, deinen Kundendatensatz dort zu löschen (deine GameChest-Benutzer-ID mit Kauf- und Abo-Verlauf). Wird die Anfrage nicht gestellt oder schlägt sie fehl, wird dein Konto trotzdem gelöscht; unser Server merkt sich die offene Anfrage (nur deine GameChest-Benutzer-ID) und wiederholt sie automatisch alle 6 Stunden, höchstens 7 Tage lang. Der Vermerk wird 30 Tage nach der bestätigten Löschung bei RevenueCat entfernt. Lässt sich der Datensatz auch so nicht löschen, löschen wir ihn bei RevenueCat von Hand; du kannst uns auch schreiben, dann tun wir das innerhalb von 30 Tagen. Das Löschen des Kontos kündigt ein laufendes Abo nicht (3.6). Apple und Google bewahren eigene Kaufbelege nach eigenen Regeln auf.
  • Tills Tageszähler: ein täglicher Job löscht ihn, sobald er älter als 31 Tage ist, und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst (3.10).
  • Sucheingaben: nicht in einer Datenbank und nicht zu deinem Konto gespeichert; Suchbegriff und Katalogtreffer bleiben höchstens 5 Minuten im Arbeitsspeicher der Serverfunktion und werden dann gelöscht (3.3).
  • Von dir gemeldete Till-Antworten: bis zu 180 Tage, mit deinem Konto früher gelöscht. Anthropic löscht Chatdaten binnen 30 Tagen (3.10).
  • Foto-KI: Foto und Text werden nicht gespeichert; den täglichen Fotozähler löscht ein täglicher Job, sobald er älter als 31 Tage ist, und sofort, wenn du dein Konto löschst (3.12).
  • Daten auf deinem Gerät: bis du dich abmeldest, die Demo-Daten löschst oder die App löschst.
